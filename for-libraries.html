Ready to Use in Your Library or Classroom

This site was built to work alongside library services — not replace them. Every resource here is free, requires no login, no download, and works on any computer or phone. Here's everything you need to run a session or share this with your patrons.

What This Site Offers Libraries

Both Arapahoe Libraries and Aurora Public Library do excellent technology work — but neither currently offers a self-paced digital literacy curriculum patrons can follow at home or at a library computer independently. That's the gap this site fills.

🛤️

Session Formats

You can run a one-off session or build these into a recurring program. Here are three ready-to-use formats:

Format Time Best For What to Use
Drop-In Introduction 30 min Seniors, first-time patrons Open one Module (e.g., Avoid Scams). Walk through together on the projector. Let participants try the Scam Simulator on their own computers.
Guided Pathway Session 60 min Adults, job seekers, parents Choose a pathway (Safety or Jobs). Walk through Lessons 1–3 as a group. Assign Lessons 4–5 as homework. Share the site URL for at-home review.
Ongoing Digital Literacy Series Weekly, 45 min Committed learners, community groups One pathway per 5-week series. One lesson per week, with 15 minutes of group discussion. Use the quizzes as a check-in at the start of each session.
AI Literacy Workshop New 60 min Adults, seniors, staff training Open the AI Literacy path. Complete Lessons 1–3 together (understanding AI, using AI tools, spotting deepfakes). Discuss one real example from local news. Continue with Lessons 4–5 independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does anything require a library card or login?

No. Everything on this site — every lesson, quiz, and tool — is free and accessible without any account. Patrons can use it directly on library computers, on their phones, or at home.

Can we add a link to this site from our library website?

Absolutely — that's exactly what we're asking for. A link from your Technology Help or Digital Literacy resources page would help the most people find it. No permission needed.

Can I use this content in my sessions without crediting the site?

Yes. This is a student-led community project and the goal is impact, not credit. If you adapt any content for your own printed materials, a small 'adapted from technologymadesimple.org' note is appreciated but not required.

Can someone from your team come run a session at our branch?

Yes — reach out using the form below and we'll do our best to schedule a visit to your branch. We're local to the Centennial/Aurora area.

How is the AI Literacy path different from other resources?

Most digital literacy resources were built before the current wave of AI tools and don't cover topics like deepfakes, AI privacy risks, or how to use ChatGPT safely. This path is written specifically for general audiences who want practical guidance, not technical theory.

Is this affiliated with any library or school?

This is an independent student-led project, not officially affiliated with any library or district. We're happy to discuss formal partnerships if there's a mutual interest.

Ready to partner or have a question?

We'd love to bring a session to your library, add a resource to your website, or simply hear what would make this more useful for your patrons.
