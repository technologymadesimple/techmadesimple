Online Safety Path

I Want to Stay Safe Online

You don't have to be a tech expert to protect yourself online — you just need to know what to watch for. These 8 lessons cover the most important safety habits, in plain language, with no jargon.

✓ Spot a scam before it's too late ✓ Create passwords that actually protect you ✓ Browse the web safely ✓ Know exactly what to do if something goes wrong
01

How Scams Work — and How to Spot Them

Scammers use email, text, and phone calls to trick people into giving away money or personal information. Learn the patterns they follow so you can recognise them instantly.

⏱ 7 min All Ages
→
02

Creating Strong Passwords

Most people use passwords that are easy to guess. Learn what makes a password strong, how to remember it, and why using the same one everywhere is dangerous.

⏱ 6 min All Ages
→
03

Safe Browsing Habits

Not every website is trustworthy. Learn how to check whether a site is safe, what warning signs to look for in links, and what to do when something looks suspicious.

⏱ 7 min All Ages
→
04

Protecting Your Personal Information

Your name, address, phone number, and date of birth are valuable to scammers. Learn what's safe to share online, what to keep private, and how apps collect your data.

⏱ 8 min All Ages
→
05

Phone & Text Message Scams

Scammers increasingly target people through phone calls and text messages. Learn how to spot fake package alerts, bank warnings, robocalls, and urgent requests before they trick you.

⏱ 8 min All Ages
→
06

Social Media Safety

Social media can be a great way to stay connected, but it also creates opportunities for scammers. Learn how to protect your privacy, avoid fake accounts, and stay safe online.

⏱ 7 min All Ages
→
07

AI Scams & Deepfakes

Artificial intelligence is making scams more convincing than ever. Learn how AI-generated voices, images, videos, and messages can be used to deceive people—and how to protect yourself.

⏱ 8 min All Ages
→
08

What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

Think you've been scammed? Clicked a bad link? This lesson walks you through exactly what to do — step by step — and who to contact for help.

⏱ 6 min All Ages
→
