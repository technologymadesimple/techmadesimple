Write a clear, polite email

Practice writing to a teacher, librarian, or community organization. This is an email situation; no emails will be sent.

Quick email etiquette guide
  • Greeting: “Hello Ms. Rivera,” or “Dear Dr. Patel,”
  • Purpose: Explain why you are writing in the first sentence or two.
  • Tone: Use complete sentences, polite language, and avoid texting abbreviations.
  • Closing: “Thank you,” or “Sincerely,” followed by your name.
  • Before sending: Check spelling, the recipient, and any attachment.
