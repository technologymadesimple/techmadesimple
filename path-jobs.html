I Want to Apply for Jobs Online

Most jobs today are advertised and applied for online. This path walks you through everything you need — from setting up an email address to submitting your first application and preparing for a video interview.

Keep a simple tracker

Write down each job you apply for — company name, date applied, and any follow-up date. A simple notebook works fine. Staying organised makes follow-ups much easier and shows professionalism.
